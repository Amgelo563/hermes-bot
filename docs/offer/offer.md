# 💹 Offer

The offer feature allows users to create, manage and search service offers.

## 💻 Available commands

All command names, descriptions and options are editable through
the [💹 Offer Messages](offer-messages.md) file.

In the default configuration they are named:

- `/offer` - To create an offer.
- `/offers` - The main offer management command.
  - `/offers info` - Shows information about a given offer.
  - `/offers update` - Updates your offers. Also usable by staff.
  - `/offers delete` - Deletes your offers. Also usable by staff.
  - `/offers repost` - Reposts your offers.
  - `/offers search` - Searches for offers that match given criteria.
