# ✅ Missing Requirement Placeholders

The missing requirement placeholders extract information about an unfulfilled requirement, and are used on the template
of the `deny` or `warn` embeds. They start with `%missingRequirement_...%`.

| **Option**      | **Description**                      | **Result example**                                     |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| **name**        | The requirement's field name.        | Bad word detected                                      |
| **description** | The requirement's field description. | A bad word was detected in your service's description. |
